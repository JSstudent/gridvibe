{# The three-outcome close prompt for one session group. Live terminals are memory-only, so one misclick must not end them silently: Cancel keeps the session, "Save and close" writes a reusable preset first and closes only if that succeeded, and "Close session" is the plain close. Anything that is not one of those three presses (Escape, the backdrop) keeps the session. Shared by the workspace window, where the session tab's × opens it, and by the agent dashboard, where a session card's × does.
