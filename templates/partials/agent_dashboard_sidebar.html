{# The agent dashboard, docked to the workspace. The same tree the dialog shows (`partials/agent_dashboard_dialog.html`), in a column beside the grid rather than a surface over it — for the reader who wants to see what is running *while* they work rather than stopping to ask. `dashboard-sidebar.js` owns everything inside `#agentSidebarBody`, and takes every field on a row from `dashboard-dialog.js`, so the two surfaces cannot disagree about the same pane. It is chrome, not a dialog: it sits inside `.workspace-body` in the flow, it is toggled by the button at the head of the session tab line, and its open/shut state belongs to the workspace and is saved with it. So there is no scrim, no `.modal-shell`, and no Escape handler — nothing here is over anything. Workspace page only: there is no grid to dock to on the launcher. #}
